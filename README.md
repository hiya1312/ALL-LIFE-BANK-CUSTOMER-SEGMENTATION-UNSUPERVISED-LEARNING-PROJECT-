# ALL-LIFE-BANK-CUSTOMER-SEGMENTATION-UNSUPERVISED-LEARNING-PROJECT-
The primary objective of this project is to identify different 
segments within the existing customer base based on their spending 
patterns and past interactions with the bank. By segmenting 
customers, the bank can: 
1. Understand Customer Behavior: Gain insights into how 
different groups of customers interact with the bank, their 
spending habits, and their preferences. 
2. Personalize Marketing Campaigns: Tailor marketing strategies 
to target specific customer segments with relevant offers, 
promotions, and communication. 
3. Improve Service Delivery: Identify areas where customer 
support can be enhanced to address the specific needs of 
different customer groups. 
4. Enhance Customer Retention: By understanding the needs and 
behaviors of different segments, the bank can take proactive 
steps to retain valuable customers and reduce churn.
